        Perfect Parry
        Timing battle
        Perfect Parry
        • Monsters attack in combos. Watch each one closely: it pulls back, flashes a ✦ glint, then swings. Tap PARRY right as the hit lands.
        • Parry every hit in a combo to counterattack!
        • Red attacks can’t be parried. DODGE those instead.
        • Sneaky attacks don’t flash a glint, so you’ll have to read the monster’s moves. Turn the sound on: every glint goes “shing!”
        • Each parry gives you ⚡ energy for a Special attack or a Heal.
        • Keyboard: Space = parry, Shift or K = dodge, 1–3 = actions.
